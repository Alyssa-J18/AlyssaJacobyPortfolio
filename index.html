Blender Inc. Summer Internship 2026

My 2026 summer internship was with the marketing agency Blender Inc.

Over the course of my internship I created three social media series of three posts each: two for their clients and one for Blender Inc. itself.

Get the Lead Out Pittsburgh

This social series for Get the Lead Out Pittsburgh was made for their 2026 National Lead Poisoning Awareness Week info series.

The process began with deciding on a theme, then implementing the design while incorporating facts from their website to cover the topics of “Get Your Home Tested,” “Get Your Child Tested,” and "Get the Facts.”

An additional Instagram story video was created from drone footage I filmed myself and was edited in Adobe After Effects.

Each post was adapted to work in Instagram's square, 3:4, and story formats.

This project used Canva as its main design application.

Get the Facts Story Video

Staunton Farm Foundation

For my second project, I created a social series for the Staunton Farm Foundation promoting Suicide Prevention Awareness Month.

This process involved designing a single post in three distinct styles. After meeting with my mentor, we chose one, and that style was used to create the other two posts.

I also wrote the copy that accompanied these posts.

This project used Adobe InDesign as its main design application.

Blender Inc.

For my final social series for my internship, I created a client spotlight template for Blender Inc. to highlight their clients.

This process began with bouncing ideas back and forth with my mentor and our team. I then created a few design ideas for them to choose from, and two different styles were made for this spotlight theme. I was also tasked with creating a post schedule, which involved researching their clients and choosing the best month to highlight each.

This project used Adobe Illustrator and InDesign as its main design applications.

Pittsburgh Sports League 25th Anniversary Logo

For their 25th anniversary season, the Pittsburgh Sports League reached out to me to design a new 25th anniversary logo for them.

This process involved looking at their old logos and coming up with a new design that highlighted Pittsburgh and PSL at the same time. Multiple mockups were made and sent to the client, who chose between a few and asked for revisions. Eventually, the final design was chosen and printed onto shirts for their sports leagues to wear on game nights.

I am also currently working with PSL to design merchandise.

This project used Adobe Photoshop as its main design application.

Pitt Football Design Deck

For my final project in my Integrating Writing and Design class at the University of Pittsburgh, I chose to create a pitch deck for the Pitt Football team breaking down a new visual theme which I called “Breakthrough.”

This project allowed me to study how the University of Pittsburgh creates its own visual style guides, and I followed their trends and themes to create my own. I created three visual asset examples to showcase how the “Breakthrough” theme should look and feel. I also curated specific overlays and inclusions that fit my theme to serve as assets.

This project used Adobe InDesign as its main design application.

Capstone Project: Mr. Pickleball

For my ongoing senior capstone project, I have decided to create a fake pickleball brand. I am creating the entire brand from scratch to cover any topics in graphic design that my previous classes may not have taught.
